STEP 3. CONVERSION — THE RULES
People
- One person — one row of the "Clients" sheet, even if they are on many sheets and in different years.
- Join one person: by phone, then by Telegram nick, then by full name (case, «ё» and word order do not matter). Join by name only if there is one such person and the records have no different phones or nicks.
- A full name in one cell: one word — "First name"; otherwise the first word is "Last name", the second "First name", the rest "Middle name". In Latin letters: the first word is "Last name in Latin", the rest "First name in Latin".
- Remarks after the name — "(Anna's colleague)", "from Olga T." — are not part of the name: put them on the "Notes" sheet. Drop the remark "(again)".
- Several people in one cell ("Anna + wife + son", "Anna and Peter"):
  - everyone with a name gets their own client and case; the family's cases get one code in the "Family" column;
  - for those without a name write a note to the main client ("applies with wife and son") and list them in the report.
- A consent and a contract are carried over only with a date. "Consent: yes" without a date is not carried over; list it in the report.
Contacts
- Phone — international, without spaces: +375291234567. Belarusian numbers "8 029 …", "029 …", "29 …" → +375 29 …; the same for codes 25, 33, 44. A number of another country keeps its code. A number you cannot make sense of is not carried over; list it in the report.
- Telegram — only the nick: "@anna_iv", "anna_iv", "t.me/anna_iv" → @anna_iv. Text like "on Viber" or "no" is not Telegram. If there is a number next to it and it says Viber or WhatsApp, fill "Messenger" and "Messenger contact"; otherwise — a note.
- Email — one address. A cell "email password" ("anna@gmail.com Pa55word"): the address is the "Login" on the "Accounts" sheet (the system for email), the rest is the "Password". Anything else in such a cell (a link, a comment) goes to the account's "Note".
- A cell that mixes several things ("anna@mail.ru URN: ITA123456789012") — split it into columns: the email to the email, the number to "Visa center number".
Empty values
- "-", "—", "no", empty and 0 in a money column — an empty cell. Never write "-" or "no" into the file instead of an empty cell.
Dates — into the file as real dates (date cells) or as text DD.MM.YYYY
- Numbers like 45678 in a date column are Excel dates: convert them.
- "17.12 2026", "17/12/2026", "until 25.12.2026" → 17.12.2026 and 25.12.2026. Drop the time in a cell ("17.08.2026 at 15.57").
- Marked:
  - a date without a year "17.08" — the year from the sheet's name; if the name has no year, ask me;
  - a month with a year ("August 2025") — the 1st of the month; a month without a year ("March") — the year from the sheet's name;
  - a consultation without a date — the 1st of the month from a column like "Month of request".
- A year alone ("2024") is not a date: do not carry it over, list it in the report.
- A date of birth in the future or before 1910 is not carried over.
Money
- A sum is a number without its currency. The currency goes into its own column as a code: BYN, EUR, USD, PLN. The CRM keeps no other currencies: a sum in another currency is not carried over; list it in the report. Without a currency — the workspace's base currency ("About" sheet of the template; if there is none — BYN).
- A case's "Price" is only in the base currency. If the price is only in another currency ("Price in złoty"), do not convert it: leave it empty and list it in the report.
- What the client has already paid for a case: a column like "Paid", "Payment", "Deposited"; if there is none — "Price" minus "Balance"; if there is no balance either — "Prepayment". That is one row of the "Payments" sheet on the day of payment. Without a day of payment — on the submission day, else on the day of queueing or of sending the application, else on the consultation day (marked). Without any date — the payment is not carried over; list it in the report.
- If the money of one case is on two sheets (for example, on the submissions sheet and on the queue sheet), do not add them up: take the one sheet with more data. If the sums differ, ask me. The same with the talon.
- A paid consultation — "Price" and "Paid: yes" on the "Consultations" sheet, unless the spreadsheet says it was not paid (for example, a "Balance" above zero).
- A talon to the registrar ("Payment for the talon", "My expense", "Talon") → the case's "Talon", "Talon paid before CRM: yes" (old talons are already paid). The talon's currency from the spreadsheet, else the base one. A talon without a registrar is not carried over; list it in the report.
- A payout to a partner ("Percent paid") → "Partner terms: fixed", "Partner payout" — the sum, "Partner paid on" — the decision day, else the submission day (marked).
- "Profit", "Balance", "Total" are not carried over.
Cases
- A row of a submissions sheet or of "clients in work" is one case: one visa of one person. "Source" — the sheet's exact name.
- Registrar "me", "myself", "on my own", "without an intermediary" — there is no registrar, the cell is empty.
- A result in words:
  - "visa", "multi", "multiple for 2 years" → "Outcome: visa" and "Entries: multiple";
  - "single", "one-time" → single;
  - "refusal" → refusal;
  - "after appeal" → visa and "After appeal: yes";
  - "did not apply", "did not go", "declined" → not submitted.
  A result you cannot make sense of stays empty; list it in the report. Fill "Visa valid from" and "Visa valid to" only if the spreadsheet has the dates.
- The number at the visa center — "Visa center number": an ID, an application number, an assigned number, a user code (Greece). From a cell like "ID 12345 of 01.02" take only the number.
- Leave "Stage" and "Opened on" empty: the CRM works them out from the dates and the result. The exception is a case without a single date: its "Opened on" is 01.01 of the sheet's year (marked), otherwise the CRM counts it as opened today.
- Fill "Consultation code" only if the case clearly grew out of this person's consultation (its country is among the consultation's "Countries", the consultation is no later than the case's start). Otherwise empty: the CRM links them itself.
Consultations
- "Doing the visa", "Decision": "yes", "doing" → yes; "no", "not doing" → no; "thinking" → thinking. Other values — ask me.
- A column describing the request ("Case", "Details", "Comment") → the consultation's "Notes". Countries from it go to "Countries" as well.
Queue (waiting list)
- A row of a queue sheet is a row on the "Queue" sheet for this person's case in the sheet's country:
  - if the person has a case of this country on the submissions sheets, attach the row to it (the one that fits by dates: queued no later than submitted);
  - if there is no case, create one ("Visa country" — the sheet's country, "Source" — the queue sheet's name).
- Columns:
  - "Date queued", "queued" → "Queued on";
  - "Appointment date", "Letter with date", "Date of the letter with the submission date" → "Letter on";
  - "Submission date" → the case's "Submitted on";
  - "City" → the case's "Application city";
  - "Registrar" → the case's.
- Queued again ("Date queued at the new visa center") — a second "Queue" row of the same case. The first row's "Removed on" is the same day, its "Removal reason" — "moved to the new visa center".
- A comment "Removed from the queue 12.06.2026", "taken off 12.06" → "Removed on" (and the reason if there is one). Other comments go to the client's "Notes".
- "Email" and "Password" columns side by side — the client's email account (the email system) for this case. That email is also the case's "Application email" unless the spreadsheet says otherwise.
- If the submissions sheets have "Queued" and "Letter with date" columns and there is no queue sheet — also a row on the "Queue" sheet, but only for countries with a waiting list. Which countries these are — ask me if the sheet names do not show it.
Windowed applications (France)
- "Date the application was sent" — attempt 1; "60th day" → "Last day of the window" (as in the spreadsheet, do not work it out again).
- "Again" — the date of the next application, that is attempt 2. The previous attempt gets "Outcome: expired" and "Closed on" — its last day of the window. "60th day (2nd cycle)" — the last day of attempt 2; "Again (2nd cycle)" — attempt 3, and so on.
- "not sending" in a column of repeats — the last attempt gets "Outcome: cancelled".
- If the person has no case of this country, create one as for the queue.
Bookings
- A row of a bookings sheet → "Bookings" of this person's case: in the booking's country, else their latest case. Without a case the row is not carried over; list it in the report.
- A booking for several people ("Anna and Oleg") — in the first one's case; name the others in "Comment".
- Columns:
  - "cancel the booking by" → "Free cancellation until";
  - "booking email" → "Account", and the same email is a row of "Accounts" with the Booking system;
  - "Number of hotels" and the rest → "Comment".
- A booking without a hotel name gets "Booking" as its "Place"; list such rows in the report.
Emails and accounts
- An "Emails" sheet and the like are my own mailboxes and accounts: rows of "Accounts" without a "Client code", "Whose email: my backup". If the sheet shows the email is a client's — with their "Client code".
- "email made by me", "mailbox I created" → "Whose email: created by me", "Created by me: yes", and this email is the case's "Application email".
- The login and password of a visa center's site ("account details", "visa center login") → the visa center system of this country from the "Reference" (VFS Global, TLScontact, BLS…). If it is unclear which center it is — ask me.
Lists
- Countries, application cities, purposes, channels, partners, registrars, systems — values from the template's "Reference", spelled the same. Bring synonyms to them: "word-of-mouth" → "Word of mouth" if there is such a value.
- Values missing from the "Reference":
  - for channels, partners, purposes and registrars keep my spelling, one per value — the CRM will offer to add them to its lists;
  - for countries and cities — list them in the report and invent nothing.
Codes
- "Client code" — "<sheet>-<row number>" of the first row where the person turned up ("Подача25-17").
- "Case code" and "Consultation code" — "<sheet>-<row number>" of their own row. A case created from a queue or applications sheet — also by its own row.
- Leave "Code" of the other sheets empty.
- "Client" on every sheet but "Clients" — the full name as on the "Clients" sheet: by it the CRM checks the code did not shift.

THE VISACRM FORMAT, VERSION 1
One XLSX file. The first row of every sheet holds the headers, exactly as below. The order of sheets and columns does not matter; empty columns may be left out. "*" marks a required column; do not write it in the header.
Values:
- date — a date cell or text DD.MM.YYYY;
- sum — a number;
- currency — the code BYN, EUR, USD or PLN, empty — the base currency;
- yes/no — "yes" or "no";
- phone — +375291234567;
- Telegram — @nick;
- several countries — separated by commas;
- code — any text up to 64 characters, unique on its sheet.

Clients: Client code* | Last name | First name | Middle name | Last name in Latin | First name in Latin | Phone | Telegram | Messenger (WhatsApp / Viber) | Messenger contact | Email | Date of birth | Lead channel | Partner | Consent signed (date) | Contract number | Contract date | Passport number | Personal number | Nationality | Sex (M / F / X) | Passport issued | Passport valid until | Issued by | Place of birth
At least "Last name" or "First name" is needed.

Notes: Code | Client code* | Client | Date | Text*

Consultations: Consultation code | Client code* | Client | Date* | Applicants (number) | Countries | Decision (yes / no / thinking) | Lead channel | Planned trip | Follow up on | Price | Currency | Paid (yes / no) | Paid on | Notes

Cases: Case code* | Client code* | Client | Case number | Family | Consultation code | Source | Visa country* | Country of application | Purpose | Application city | Hall (standard / premium) | Registrar | Visa center number | Application email | Whose email (client's / created by me / my backup / registrar's) | Stage (waiting / documents / submission / review / result) | Opened on | Questionnaire due | Questionnaire received | Submitted on | Outcome (visa / refusal / not submitted) | Entries (single / double / multiple) | After appeal (yes / no) | Decided on | Visa valid from | Visa valid to | Days of stay | Price | Prepayment | Consultation credit | Talon | Talon currency | Talon rate | Talon date | Talon paid before CRM (yes / no) | Partner terms (percent / fixed / no payout / not tracked) | Partner percent | Partner payout | Partner paid on (date) | Paid | Balance
"Case number", "Paid", "Balance" come only in an export from the CRM; leave them empty. "Prepayment" is the agreed prepayment for new cases; money received goes to the "Payments" sheet.

Payments: Code | Case code* | Client | Date* | Amount* | Currency | Rate | Official (yes / no) | Comment

Expenses: Code | Case code* | Client | Date* | Description* | Amount* | Currency | Rate

Queue: Code | Case code* | Client | Country | Queued on* | Letter on | Removed on | Removal reason

Windowed applications: Code | Case code* | Client | Country | Attempt number | Sent on* | Last day of the window | Outcome (date received / expired / cancelled; empty — open) | Closed on

Bookings: Code | Case code* | Client | Kind (hotel / flight) | Status (active / rescheduled / cancelled) | Place* | Check-in | Check-out | Free cancellation until | Valid until | Booking number | Account | Comment

Accounts: Code | Client code | Case code | Client | System* | Login* | Password | Whose email (client's / created by me / my backup / registrar's) | Created by me (yes / no) | Extra ID | Note
Without a "Client code" — my own account.

Fill the reference columns "Client" and "Country" on the other sheets: the CRM checks them against the codes. An empty rate — the CRM takes the latest National Bank rate the site has.
